MC Analysis & Recommendations — April 2026

Strategic Summary — March 2026

The consulting engine is running well. The product and course revenue streams are not. Total business revenue of $99,133 is a strong month — 13% above the monthly pace required to hit the $1,050,000 FY2027 target. Consulting is fully utilised and delivering. That's the good news.

The bad news: combined product and course revenue for March came in at $2,014 — against a monthly pace requirement of approximately $19,583 to hit the $235,000 FY2027 target. That's 10 cents in the dollar. Two months into Q4 FY2026 and the scalable revenue streams are not moving. The Easter Sale ran for the full month across LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram, generated reasonable social impressions, and produced $1,503 in Wix and Stripe revenue. The conversion rate from 2,061 unique visitors to 81 orders is 3.9% — which is functional — but at an average order value of $14.40, the basket is too small to drive meaningful revenue. The traffic is coming in; the problem is what people are buying, or not buying.

LinkedIn is performing above expectation as a brand and thought-leadership channel. The ADC free resource post (562 impressions, 39 clicks, 8.7% engagement) is the clearest signal of what works: free, high-value, time-sensitive compliance content drives action. The AVC award post (14.8% engagement) confirms the team's credibility content resonates. These are not flukes — they are a pattern. The Easter sale posts, by comparison, averaged under 3 clicks per post. Product promotion without a strong reason to act is not cutting through on LinkedIn.

The marketing hours situation requires an honest conversation. 70.5 hours were logged against the marketing project in March — 3.5 times the 20-hour monthly cap. Website maintenance alone consumed 27.8 hours. This is not a marketing operations problem; it is a project scope problem. The new courses page build, WordPress work, and CMS data entry are development tasks that appear to be running through the marketing project code. Before April begins, it needs to be clear what counts as marketing hours versus website development, and whether the 20-hour cap applies to both or just to promotional and content work.

⚠ Risk Flag Product and course revenue is tracking at less than 11% of the pace required to hit FY2027 targets. The AC/CC coaching programs are not yet live and generating enrolments. The Easter Sale produced minimal uplift relative to the effort invested. If this trajectory continues into EOFY, the $235,000 product + course target is not achievable. This needs a plan, not an assumption that EOFY will fix it.
April Action Plan
  • Get the AC/CC program live and generating enrolments this month. These courses are the primary scalable revenue engine. Every week they sit in build without enrolments is lost FY2027 income. Set a live date for April, brief a launch email sequence, and post to LinkedIn the moment they open.
  • Lift the average Wix order value. $14.40 average order is a register or template item. Push QMS packs, full policy suites, and the aX guide — products in the $150–$500 range — to the front of the shop and into email campaigns. Run a fortnightly product email in April targeting QMS suite and TLI41225 as the calendar requires.
  • Replicate the ADC post format for May EOFY warm-up. Free resource + compliance deadline + CTA is the highest-converting content format visible in the data. The EOFY sale prep starts Week 3 of May. Begin building that pipeline now with a compliance-themed email and LinkedIn post in Week 3–4 of April.
  • Resolve the marketing hours classification issue before April closes. Define what is marketing versus development, log it separately, and report both. The 20-hour cap cannot be managed if development work is sitting inside the marketing project code. This is a Harvest setup conversation for Jodie.
  • Confirm Vetr RCTI and add March actuals to the dashboard. Once received, check whether Vetr revenue is trending toward the $15,000 FY2027 target and whether any action is needed to drive Vetr-specific traffic or listings.
