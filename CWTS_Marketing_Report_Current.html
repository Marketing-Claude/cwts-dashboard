MC Analysis & Recommendations — May 2026

Strategic Summary — April 2026

April tells a clearer story than March. On an accrual basis — work done in April including May 1 invoices — total revenue was $73,234, representing 84% of the $87,500 monthly pace required to hit $1,050,000 for FY2027. Cash received was $70,659. The gap between accrual and cash ($2,575) reflects invoices issued on 1 May for April work, which will land in May's Xero figures. March's $99,133 cash figure was inflated by a large settlement — April is a more representative read of the baseline. Consulting at $72,550 accrual is solid but still below pace, and without a large one-off transaction, product and course revenue needs to start pulling its weight.

Product and course revenue in April was negligible on a sold basis: Wix shop $365, Vetr sold $280, aXcelerate $36. Five email campaigns sent, zero attributed orders for the second consecutive month. LinkedIn ran both organically and as a paid channel in April — organic content drove 2,836 impressions and 148 clicks at a strong 8.6% engagement rate, while the paid website visits campaign added 45,234 impressions and 503 clicks at $375.26 spend ($0.75 CPC). The QMS Essentials ad was the strongest paid creative at 1.48% CTR, absorbing 60% of the budget. Despite the combined paid and organic reach, no attributed conversions were recorded from LinkedIn in April. The audience is engaged and clicking — the gap is on the landing page and product page conversion side. Update as of 1 May: AC1 (The VET Landscape) is now live. The remaining AC2–AC12 units are in accelerated production with an offshore contractor. The EOFY sale (35% off everything, opening 1 June) includes AC1 and a full series pre-sale — this is the first real course enrolment opportunity for the coaching program.

One flag requires resolution before May spend is committed: the Xero Promotions and Advertising line shows $1,514.24 for April. Google Ads ($301.90) and LinkedIn Ads ($375.26) account for $677.16 — the remaining ~$837 is unidentified and likely includes platform subscriptions coded to this line. Clarify before May budget is finalised. On hours: the 73.5 hours logged in April is a one-off. Jess and Sandra completed a full-year marketing asset build this month — all written content, digital creatives, email campaigns, and templates planned and produced for the year ahead. This is a significant front-loaded investment that should substantially reduce the hours required in forward months. From May onward, the 20-hour monthly cap should be achievable.
